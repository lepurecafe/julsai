Local-first Markdown for macOS

Write between the lines.

Julsai keeps reading and writing in one continuous surface, with focused editing, rich document rendering, and direct access to your local files.

Focus without hiding the document

Focus Mode sharpens the current block. Typewriter Mode keeps the writing line at a comfortable height.

Rich Markdown, still lossless

Mermaid, footnotes, TOC, YAML Front Matter, math, code, tables, and images remain grounded in the original Markdown.

Two deliberate visual languages

Julsai Light and Dark are the default. The original blue, violet, and neon system remains available as Cyberpunk.

Your files stay yours

No account, ads, analytics, or remote document service. Julsai works with files and folders you choose on your Mac.
