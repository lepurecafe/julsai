Can I use my existing Markdown files?

Yes. Open the Markdown files already on your Mac, read them, and make changes. Documents are saved in the file locations you choose.

Why can’t I see my local images?

Images beside a document may need folder access. Connect the document’s folder in Julsai and check that its image paths are correct.

Where are my documents stored?

They stay in the location you choose when opening or saving a file. Julsai has no document server. If a service such as iCloud Drive syncs that location, its own settings apply.

Can I share my document as one HTML file?

In 0.5.8, one HTML export can include local images and Markdown documents linked directly from the original. If Julsai needs access, choose the folder containing the linked documents when you export. You can also export to PDF.

How do I use Daylight?

Daylight is the fifth color style in the 0.5.8 update. In that version, choose Daylight in Settings under Color Style, then choose Light, Dark, or System under Appearance. The Mac App Store lists the version currently available.
